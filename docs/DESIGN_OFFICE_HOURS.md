# Design: WonderReel Indonesia (Bilingual Safety & Anti-Piracy Edition)

Branch: main
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Startup

## 1. Problem Statement
Indonesian parents face a deep conflict: they want their children to learn English for career and cognitive benefits, but they are terrified of online cartoons. Many parents do not speak English fluently, meaning bad actors can wrap inappropriate or malicious content (e.g. "Elsagate" exploits) inside friendly animations with English dialogue, and parents cannot easily audit them.

## 2. Demand Evidence
- Strong visual hooks proven by **45 organic parent hand-raisers** requesting access to a single local demo video on social media.
- Verifiable panic and protective instincts from parents around English content safety.

## 3. Status Quo
- **Active Curating:** Parents spend hours sitting beside their kids, manually vetting videos, or searching and stitching safe content themselves.
- **Passivity:** Some accept the risk out of fatigue but carry ongoing moral guilt.

## 4. Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **The Primary Wedge:** Upwardly mobile, middle-class parents in urban centers (Jakarta, Bandung, Surabaya) who do not speak English fluently themselves, but want their children (ages 2–7) to learn.
- **The Secondary Wedge:** Lower-income families who want the same educational outcomes but have tight cellular data / bandwidth constraints.

## 5. Premises
- **Premise 1 (The Bilingual Rule):** Stories must accept Bahasa Indonesia prompts. The resulting film plays clear **English narration/audio**, but displays **Bahasa Indonesia subtitles** on screen so the parent can fully audit the video's safety in seconds.
- **Premise 2 (The Anti-Cheat Rule):** Scrapers will try to download and upload our unique generated films onto YouTube to monetize them. We must proactively watermark and brand-bundle all output videos.
- **Premise 3 (The Data Cap Rule):** Video streams must be highly optimized and cached so they do not drain Indonesian families' cellular data limits.

## 6. Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Bare-Bones MVP
- **Summary:** Only translate input, send to PixVerse, and stream the raw video.
- **Effort:** Small (CC: ~10 mins)
- **Risk:** High (Zero anti-cheat protection, no audit subtitles for parents).
- **Pros:** Extremely fast to ship.
- **Cons:** High risk of piracy; parents cannot audit the English voice.

### Approach B: Bilingual Safety & Anti-Piracy Assembly (Recommended)
- **Summary:** Build a post-processing queue using **FFmpeg** that overlays Bahasa subtitles, bakes in a semi-transparent "WonderReel" watermark, and stitches a 2-second branded outro bumper to prevent YouTube piracy. Serve files via expiring signed URLs.
- **Effort:** Medium (CC: ~2 hours / Human: ~1 day)
- **Risk:** Low
- **Pros:** Strongest IP protection; complete parent peace of mind.
- **Cons:** Slightly higher processing latency on generation.

## 7. Recommended Approach
We choose **Approach B** because it directly solves the language blindspot (via Bahasa captions + English audio) and fully safeguards your PixVerse budget against bulk copyright piracy.

## 8. Technical Architecture Details (FFmpeg Post-Processing Pipeline)
1. **Gemini Story-to-Prompt:** Receives parent Bahasa text → Screens safety → Translates prompt to English → Triggers PixVerse.
2. **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Generates English voice-over narration MP3.
3. **FFmpeg Assembly Pipeline:**
   - Overlay **bilingual subtitles** (Bahasa text, styled with clean fonts and borders).
   - Overlay a **semi-transparent WonderReel watermark** on the bottom/top corner.
   - Stitch a **2-second branded bumper outro** (`assets/outro.mp4`).
   - Compress with efficient h.264 profile capped at `540p` (max data-saving).

## 9. Success Criteria
- Zero unsafe content reaching the child.
- 100% parent auditing rate (proven by parent comprehension of what the video says).
- Bandwidth utilization under 10MB per 30-second clip.

## 10. The Assignment
